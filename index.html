FOREVER & ALWAYS

Cindy Adomaa Osei

I give you these temporary flowers as a little reminder of the depth of my love for you.
I want you to read this every day and remember just how much I love and appreciate you, baby girl. And please, show me how I can love you even better. Maybe my love hasn’t always been perfect, and maybe I haven’t always gotten everything right, but you’re the first person I’ve ever genuinely loved this deeply, and I’m truly happy that I’ve had the chance to experience this with you. I’m always filled with regret because, as much as I know I may never get to be with you forever, I never wanted you to think of me and remember the bad things.
Whenever I cross your mind, I want you to remember the beautiful moments we’ve shared, the laughter, the happiness, and all the little things that made us smile. I never wanted to become a source of painful memories for you. That’s why sometimes I cry. Not because I don’t appreciate what we’ve had, but because I regret the moments I could have handled better and the times I fell short of the love you deserved. I love you so much, Cindy.
Thank you for your kindness, your love, your patience, and everything you’ve shown me.
You’re an amazing person, and you’ll always be someone special to me. I promise to keep working on myself, to become a better person, and to learn how to love you better, not just through words but through my actions. For you, for me, and for us. I love you, baby girl. ❤️
